ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Program Objectives

The MBA-IEV curriculum is built around five transformational learning objectives — each one designed to bridge the gap between theoretical management education and real-world business leadership.

01

Build Innovation-Led Business Models

Learn to identify market gaps, design scalable solutions, and build ventures from first principles.

02

Develop an Entrepreneurial Mindset

Build resilience, decision-making abilities, and leadership skills for uncertain environments.

03

Master Venture Financing & Capital Strategy

Understand fundraising, investor relations, valuation, and capital deployment.

04

Forge Global Industry Networks

Build relationships with entrepreneurs, investors, operators, and industry leaders.

05

Lead with Purpose & Create Impact

Drive innovation through sustainability, ethical leadership, and meaningful growth.
