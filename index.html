ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Program Objectives

The MBA-IEV programme goes beyond conventional management education by helping students build real ventures, prepare for strong career opportunities, and leverage the CIIC ecosystem for mentorship, incubation, funding exposure, and industry connections.

01

Build Your Business with Seed Capital

Turn your idea into a real venture through Crescent’s practice-first MBA-IEV journey, with access to seed capital, startup building support, and opportunities to raise your own startup as part of the programme.

02

Access Strong Placement Support

Prepare for high-growth careers with personalized placement support, individual coaching, leadership development, and career guidance designed around each student’s strengths and competencies.

03

Collaborate Across Crescent’s 12 Schools

Build ideas beyond one discipline by collaborating with students and faculty across Crescent’s 12 schools, including engineering, architecture, law, pharmacy, management, arts and sciences.

04

Learn by Building, Testing & Launching

Move beyond classroom theory through capstone projects, action learning, market validation, business model creation, and venture development activities that help students build execution capability.

05

Grow with Mentors, Industry & Global Exposure

Gain exposure to industry leaders, entrepreneurs, expert mentors, career enrichment programmes, Global Immersion Program, and learning opportunities that strengthen both startup and corporate career pathways.

Build While You Learn

Learn from Founders & Operators

Access Seed Capital & Funding

Explore seed capital and funding pathways through government schemes, NGOs, angel investors and the startup ecosystem.

Incubation, Mentoring & Market Access

Use CIIC’s incubation support, co-working access and industry connections to test, launch and grow your venture.

Become More Valuable in the Job Market

Building a business sharpens your skills in sales, strategy, problem-solving, leadership and execution; making you more valuable to employers.

Keep Both Paths Open

If your venture scales, you build it. If it pivots or fails, you still graduate with stronger real-world experience and better career readiness.
